Accuracy

Spelling creators would actually type.

Generic auto-captions turn Hindi into stretched, hard-to-read spellings. PrimeSub checks every word against the meaning of the whole video, writes it the way creators write it, and flags anything it is unsure about.

Typical auto-captions

aapakee pooree jindagee

sirph kuch knpaniya

kntrol karatee hain

PrimeSub

aapki puri zindagi

sirf kuch companies

control karti hain

Illustrative example. No tool is perfect, which is why PrimeSub highlights uncertain words so you can confirm them quickly.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is it really free?

During the private beta, yes. Each invited creator gets a limited number of free minutes.

Is my video uploaded to your servers?

The video file is processed in your browser. For transcription, only the audio track is sent to our AI transcription provider.

How accurate are the captions?

Strong on Hindi and English, but no tool is perfect. PrimeSub flags words it is unsure about so you can confirm them in seconds.

How long can a video be?

During the beta, up to 5 minutes per video.

Can I use my own font and colours?

Yes. The Brand Kit lets you upload a font you are licensed to use and set your own text and highlight colours.
